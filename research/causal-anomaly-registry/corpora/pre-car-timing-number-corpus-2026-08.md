# Pre-CAR Timing and Number Corpus — August 2026

## Scope and cutoff

This artifact preserves timing/number observations and hypotheses that existed before the Cross-Phenomenon Causal Anomaly Registry (CAR) was introduced. The contamination cutoff is 2026-08-29T03:48:01Z, the creation time of PR #179 that introduced CAR. Material first interpreted after that cutoff is not promoted into the pre-CAR hypothesis set.

This is a retrospective hypothesis-generation corpus. It is not a CAR case, does not establish anomalous information, and must not be used to score a prospective Future Information Protocol trial.

## Frozen pre-CAR timing hypothesis

The governance timing protocol created on 2026-08-12 fixed the primary repeated-minute class as `:11`, `:22`, `:33`, `:44`, and `:55`. It defined exact milestone/discovery timestamps as the primary population, excluded approximate/reconstructed timestamps from the inferential sample, specified a uniform development null of 5/60 plus an empirical activity baseline, required at least 20 exact milestone events before interpretation, and prohibited changing the hit rule after observing results without versioning the change as exploratory.

The pre-CAR registry separately preserved the user's longitudinal recurrence report. That report is evidence of the prior hypothesis, not independent proof of recurrence.

## Ordinary-activity baseline

The pre-CAR baseline contains 100 GitHub commit timestamps from 2026-08-09 through 2026-08-12. Seven rows fell in the primary repeated-minute class: 13:55:26, 15:44:25, 18:11:24, 17:22:49, 17:22:28, 16:55:48, and 16:55:15 EDT.

The original row-level baseline was therefore 7/100 = 7%. That figure is retained as the historical row-level result only.

### Independence correction

GitHub objects are not independent observations when one activity burst emits multiple records. Two repeated-minute pairs in the baseline are visibly clustered: 17:22:49 and 17:22:28 are 21 seconds apart; 16:55:48 and 16:55:15 are 33 seconds apart. Thus the seven repeated-minute rows contain only five distinct repeated-minute time episodes at sub-minute resolution.

This does **not** justify replacing 7/100 with 5/100. The denominator must also be episode-deduplicated. Because no episode-gap threshold was preregistered, any episode analysis must report sensitivity across multiple gap rules (recommended exploratory gaps: 1, 5, 10, and 15 minutes) rather than selecting a favorable threshold after inspection.

## Generator taxonomy

- `clock_timestamp`: constrained by civil time; observation can be influenced by when a person/device checks or records time.
- `github_commit_timestamp`: generated by workflow activity; multiple commits can belong to one burst and require episode deduplication.
- `github_pr_number`: sequential database identifier; once a repository reaches 333 it necessarily passed 111 and 222, so identifier recurrence alone is not anomalous.
- `human_selected_number`: contaminated for external-signal testing unless selection is independently constrained.
- `independently_randomized_target`: preferred generator for Future Information testing because the target can be created only after prediction commitment.

## Repeated-identifier semantic case: JPV-OS #333

JPV-OS PR #333 is a conspicuous retrospective semantic alignment: its title completed a "holy trinity" device inventory. However, PR #332 had already created the "holy trinity runtime" architecture and explicitly recorded the business iPhone as the remaining pending evidence intake. PR #333 then completed that already-defined third component.

Classification: **semantic coincidence with ordinary sequential provenance**. The subject matter was materially determined before identifier #333 was assigned, but the adjacent-PR dependency explains why the completion landed on the next sequential number. It is hypothesis-generating, not evidence of an anomalous information channel.

## What genuinely predates CAR

1. The primary repeated-minute class `:11/:22/:33/:44/:55`.
2. The requirement to compare meaningful timestamps against ordinary activity rather than count notable hits alone.
3. The minimum n=20 exact-milestone interpretation gate.
4. The explicit boundary that timing deviation would not establish a spiritual, causal, technological, psychological, or other mechanism by itself.
5. A user-reported longitudinal recurrence claim, preserved as report rather than statistical conclusion.

## What is not frozen as pre-CAR evidence

- Modern angel-number semantic dictionaries.
- A universal mapping such as `333 = trinity`, `555 = transition`, etc., unless a separately timestamped pre-cutoff artifact is recovered.
- Post-hoc links among the candle, six-month incubation, propagation, thresholds, and number meanings.
- Any claim that a repeated number predicted a later independently generated outcome.

Those remain exploratory hypotheses until prospectively specified.

## Handoff to CAR / Future Information Protocol

Historical records may generate hypotheses but may not score the prospective test. A qualifying future-information trial must use the existing `JPV-LABS-FIP-001` requirements: preregistration, immutable commitment before target generation, at least 128 bits of target entropy, at least two independent timestamp authorities, independently audited random target generation, blinding until target lock, an exact-match primary endpoint, and a stopping rule fixed before the first trial.

The first scientifically material escalation is not another repeated-number occurrence. It is a prospectively committed prediction that contains information about an independently generated future target beyond the specified chance model and survives independent replication.
