## Broader root investigation

### A. Literal non-human beings hypothesis

Status: NOT SUPPORTED BY CURRENT VERIFIED EVIDENCE.

The U.S. Department of Defense and AARO publicly report that, after review of historical records, classified and unclassified archives, interviews, and current UAP casework, they have found no verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial beings, activity, technology, hidden extraterrestrial exploitation programs, or government/private possession of extraterrestrial technology. Many unresolved UAP reports remain unresolved because available data are insufficient, not because non-human origin has been established.

Primary sources:
- U.S. Department of Defense, 2024-03-08, Historical Record Report summary: https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/
- U.S. Department of Defense, 2024-11-14, FY2024 UAP report discussion: https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3965403/dod-examining-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena/
- AARO: https://www.aaro.mil/

This does not prove non-human intelligence cannot exist. It means the current public evidentiary record does not support the specific claim that non-human beings govern the world.

### B. Non-human governance as legal/institutional abstraction

Status: VERIFIED CATEGORY; POSSIBLE SEMANTIC INTERPRETATION, NOT PROOF OF THE TIKTOK'S INTENDED MEANING.

U.S. law explicitly recognizes non-human legal persons. Corporations and similar juridical entities are artificial persons capable of owning property, entering contracts, suing and being sued, persisting independently of any single human, and exercising rights and duties through human agents. This establishes a literal legal sense in which powerful decision-making entities are "not humans," even though their actions ultimately originate in human-designed institutions and human agents.

Sources:
- Cornell Legal Information Institute, `legal person`: https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/legal_person
- Cornell Legal Information Institute, `artificial person`: https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/artificial_person
- Cornell Legal Information Institute, `corporation`: https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/corporation

### C. Non-human governance as automated-system influence

Status: VERIFIED SYSTEMIC MECHANISM; NOT AUTONOMOUS WORLD GOVERNMENT.

Automated systems already perform consequential routing, market execution, information dissemination, and administrative decision functions. Regulators treat system failures as capable of harming people outside the system operator itself. SEC records show that automated systems are integral to securities-market order routing, execution, data dissemination, and trade processing, while public-sector governance frameworks explicitly regulate automated decisions because they can affect rights, services, and institutional outcomes.

Sources:
- SEC, Regulation Systems Compliance and Integrity: https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2015/12/regulation-systems-compliance-integrity
- SEC, Technology and Trading Roundtable: https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2012/08/technology-trading-roundtable
- OECD STIP Compass, Canada Directive on Automated Decision-Making: https://stip.oecd.org/stip/interactive-dashboards/policy-initiatives/2025%2Fdata%2FpolicyInitiatives%2F24240

Finding: algorithms can materially govern allocation and routing without being conscious or sovereign actors. This is a stronger real-world mechanism than the literal "beings run the world" claim and should be tested wherever a source uses "non-human" ambiguously.

### D. Antarctica / resources / governance

Status: VERIFIED HUMAN INTERGOVERNMENTAL GOVERNANCE; NO VERIFIED NON-HUMAN GOVERNING ACTOR.

The Antarctic Treaty System provides a concrete governance structure. Twenty-nine Consultative Parties currently participate in decision-making; measures are adopted by consensus. The Environmental Protocol designates Antarctica as a natural reserve devoted to peace and science and prohibits Antarctic mineral-resource activities other than scientific research. The Treaty Secretariat explicitly states that neither the Treaty nor the Protocol expires in 2048 and that removing the mineral-resource prohibition would require a binding legal regime plus consensus conditions.

Primary sources:
- Antarctic Treaty Secretariat, ATCM and Other Meetings: https://www.ats.aq/e/atcm.html
- Antarctic Treaty Secretariat, Environmental Protocol: https://www.ats.aq/e/protocol.html
- Antarctic Treaty Secretariat, Resolution 3 (2023), reaffirming the mineral-resource prohibition: https://www.ats.aq/devAS/Meetings/Measure/807

Finding: for the screenshot's visible word `resources`, the verified Antarctic governance mechanism is human interstate governance constrained by treaty and environmental law, not a demonstrated hidden non-human authority.

### E. A genuinely relevant "non-human representation" thread exists, but it is political philosophy rather than hidden-being evidence

Status: VERIFIED SCHOLARLY PROPOSAL.

A 2026 Oxford University Press book by Alejandra Mancilla explicitly examines extending Antarctic political/legal representation to non-human animals and potentially to Antarctica itself. The proposal is a normative governance model—humans constructing institutions that represent non-human interests—not evidence that non-human beings presently govern the world.

Sources:
- Oxford Academic, *From Sovereignty to Guardianship: Governing Antarctica, Governing the World*: https://academic.oup.com/book/62732
- Chapter 5, `Representing Sentient Antarctica`: https://academic.oup.com/book/62732/chapter/562667670
- Chapter 6, `Nothing About Antarctica Without Antarctica`: https://academic.oup.com/book/62732/chapter/562667857

This is important because social-media content can collapse distinct meanings of "non-human governance": literal non-human intelligence, legal artificial persons, automated systems, representation of animals/nature, and human institutions acting through abstractions.
